Estimated tax (CGT)

Worst-case estimate: assumes you sell everything at once, using a single R 40,000 annual exclusion.

The R 40,000 exclusion resets every SA tax year (1 March–end Feb) and covers the GAIN, not the amount sold. By realising gains gradually and keeping each year's gain under R 40,000, you can often pay little or no CGT.

Tax-gain harvesting: sell and immediately rebuy to crystallise the gain (using up that year's exclusion) and reset your base cost higher — shrinking future taxable gains. The exclusion is shared across all your capital gains for the year, and brokerage/spread costs apply each time.

General info, not tax advice — confirm with a tax practitioner.
